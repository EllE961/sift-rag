How an answer is made

  1. BM25Exact-term evidence from an inverted index over Porter-stemmed terms.
  2. Dense · HNSWMeaning over wording: bge-small embeddings searched through a navigable small-world graph.
  3. FusionReciprocal-rank fusion merges both lists without calibrating their scores.
  4. RerankA cross-encoder reads query and passage together; blended with the fused score.
  5. AnswerClaude answers from the top five, citing exact character spans of the sources.
